Innovating for
Rural Empowerment

Gaon Gotha is a transformative initiative by The Baap Company aimed at fostering
sustainable rural development through a crowdfunded dairy farm, an old age home,
and an employment program for local youth.

The FPC Model

Gaon Gotha follows a circular economy model, where every element feeds into the next, creating a sustainable cycle of growth. From fodder production to organic fertilizers, dairy processing to bio-energy, each FPC is a pillar that strengthens rural livelihoods while integrating them into the global economy.
